\begin{abstract}
Modern artificial intelligence remains divided between continuous neural networks, which suffer from floating-point drift and catastrophic forgetting, and discrete symbolic solvers, which face combinatorial search explosion. In this paper, we demonstrate that enforcing a single uncompromising constraint---\textbf{exact 0-bit lossless conservation}---causes these two paradigms to collapse into a single uniform computing primitive: the \textbf{Monoidal Lifting Triangle (MLT)}, implemented as a cache-aligned 64-byte C99 structure with zero heap allocation and no floating-point math library dependencies.

By establishing an exact integer dual-chart isomorphism between orthogonal modular arithmetic ($\mathbb{Z}/2^{16}\mathbb{Z}$) and Log-Polar Fourier Holographic Reduced Representations (FHRR) via 7.5~KB of L1-resident lookup tables, our primitive naturally reproduces the Frobenius axioms of the ZX-calculus (Z-spider, X-spider, and Hadamard chart transitions) without prior theoretical imposition. We show that: (1) 64-layer bidirectional lifting ladders achieve exact 0-bit state reconstruction ($\mu^{64} \circ \Delta^{64} = \mathrm{id}$) at 2.54--2.75~G cells/s on ARM64 NEON; (2) dynamic currying crystallizes Boolean and arithmetic circuits directly into algebraic normal form (ANF), enabling backtrack-free $O(N)$ causal abduction over $2^{127}$-state spaces (64-bit adders in 127 steps) and accelerating 1-UIP conflict-driven clause learning; (3) local 2-morphism holonomy updates train 256-dimensional, 64-layer networks with $O(1)$ activation memory and deterministic multi-thread equivalence (\texttt{1T == 10T}); and (4) extending the 2-simplex ($\Delta^2$) to 3D tetrahedral meshes ($\Delta^3$) unifies forward deduction, abduction, and parameter learning into a single inner horn-filling operation ($\Lambda^2_k \to \Delta^2$), while enabling blind topological self-introspection via $H^2$ monopole detection and Pachner moves---yielding a self-organizing circuit topology that serves directly as its own verifiable bidirectional Abstract Syntax Tree (AST).
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

\subsection{The Precision--Abstraction Dilemma in Neural-Symbolic Systems}
Contemporary machine intelligence is split across two incompatible computational foundations. Continuous deep learning architectures excel at perceptual representation and gradient-based adaptation over high-dimensional spaces, yet they rely on non-associative floating-point arithmetic (FP32, FP16, and BF16). Across deep compositions or iterative state transitions, mantissa truncation and rounding noise accumulate irreversibly, destroying exact state reversibility, masking subtle topological invariants, and forcing systems to rely on brittle post-hoc explanations. Conversely, discrete symbolic solvers and formal proof assistants guarantee logical soundness, but they operate over rigid combinatorial search spaces that suffer from exponential branching ($O(2^N)$) and struggle to integrate continuous sensory signals.

Most existing neuro-symbolic architectures attempt to bridge this divide externally by coupling an approximate neural front-end to a separate symbolic reasoning engine. Such hybrid pipelines treat continuous signal processing, logical deduction, learning, and self-explanation as distinct software layers glued together by ad-hoc interfaces. As a result, internal neural activations cannot be inverted without information loss, and symbolic justifications remain decoupled from the underlying physical state transitions.

\subsection{Independent Convergence on Minimal Computational Primitives}
In this work, we take the opposite approach: rather than combining existing continuous and discrete engines, we ask what computational architecture emerges when a single uniform circuit primitive is subjected to \textbf{strict 0-bit lossless conservation}. Specifically, we require that every elementary state transformation be exactly invertible over finite modular integer rings, with zero floating-point arithmetic, zero dynamic heap allocation, and zero information dissipation across arbitrary circuit depths.

Remarkably, pursuing this purely engineering and algebraic constraint leads---without prior theoretical imposition---to an independent convergence with the fundamental building blocks of categorical quantum mechanics and higher-dimensional simplicial homotopy theory:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Emergent ZX-Calculus Frobenius Algebras:} Representing states simultaneously in orthogonal modular coordinates ($\mathbb{Z}/2^{16}\mathbb{Z}$) and Log-Polar Fourier Holographic Reduced Representations (FHRR) turns linear superposition and multiplicative phase binding into complementary Frobenius spiders (X-spider and Z-spider), linked by a fast L1-resident dual-chart Hadamard transition ($H$).
    \item \textbf{Dagger Compact Closed Reversibility:} Embedding dual-chart transformations inside bipartite and tripartite Feistel lifting ladders guarantees that every forward inference pass $\Delta$ admits an exact two's-complement inverse $\mu$ satisfying $\mu \circ \Delta = \mathrm{id}$ with 0-bit error, even when intermediate chart projections involve quantization rounding.
    \item \textbf{Simplicial Horn Filling and Self-Explaining Topology:} Organizing three complementary operations (Z, X, and H) on the vertices of a 2-simplex ($\Delta^2$) unifies forward deduction, causal abduction, and parameter learning into a single inner horn-filling operation ($\Lambda^2_k \hookrightarrow \Delta^2$). Extending this primitive to 3D tetrahedral meshes ($\Delta^3$) allows the circuit to detect topological obstructions ($H^1$ vortices and $H^2$ monopoles) and repair its own wiring via Pachner moves. Because every connection satisfies strict conservation, the self-organized circuit topology serves directly as a verifiable bidirectional Abstract Syntax Tree (AST).

\subsection{Exact Integer Dual Charts}
To unify continuous wave-like interference and discrete symbolic binding within a single deterministic register width, each signal channel is represented across two isomorphic integer coordinate charts:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Orthogonal Cartesian Chart ($\mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{cart}}$):} Pairs of signed 16-bit two's-complement integers $(re, im) \in (\mathbb{Z}/2^{16}\mathbb{Z})^2$, representing complex amplitudes in a discrete Gaussian-like modular ring. Component-wise addition ($re_1 \oplus re_2,\, im_1 \oplus im_2 \pmod{2^{16}}$) is strictly associative, commutative, and invertible via two's-complement negation ($\ominus$).
    \item \textbf{Log-Polar FHRR Chart ($\mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{log}}$):} Pairs $(\rho, \theta)$, where $\rho \in \mathbb{Z}$ is a signed fixed-point $Q7.8$ logarithmic magnitude ($\rho = \lfloor 256 \log_2 r \rceil$, spanning $-14$ to $+1.5$ octaves) and $\theta \in \mathbb{Z}/2^{16}\mathbb{Z}$ is a cyclic phase angle ($2\pi \leftrightarrow 2^{16}$, giving an angular resolution of $360^\circ / 65536 \approx 0.00549^\circ$). The Cartesian origin $(0, 0)$ is mapped to an explicit absorbing bottom element $\bot$ (\texttt{LC\_LOG\_ZERO} $= -32768$), while maximum amplitude saturates at $\rho_{\max} = +32767$.
\end{enumerate}

In the Log-Polar chart, holographic binding ($\circledast$) between two non-bottom signals $(\rho_1, \theta_1)$ and $(\rho_2, \theta_2)$ reduces to exact integer addition:
\begin{equation}
    (\rho_1, \theta_1) \circledast (\rho_2, \theta_2) = \bigl(\mathrm{sat}_{16}(\rho_1 + \rho_2),\; (\theta_1 + \theta_2) \bmod 2^{16}\bigr),
\end{equation}
with $\bot \circledast x = x \circledast \bot = \bot$. Unbinding ($\oslash$) is performed by subtracting $\rho_2$ and adding the modular phase inverse $(-\theta_2) \bmod 2^{16}$.

To eliminate floating-point transcendental functions (\texttt{atan2}, \texttt{log2}, \texttt{sin}, \texttt{cos}) and guarantee bit-identical execution across scalar C99 and ARM64 NEON SIMD hardware, bidirectional chart transitions are implemented using \textbf{7.5~KB of L1D-resident lookup tables (LUTs)}:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Forward Map $\Phi_{\mathrm{C}\to\mathrm{L}}: \mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{cart}} \to \mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{log}}$ (\texttt{cart\_to\_log}):} For any non-zero $(re, im) \in [-32768, 32767]^2$, the squared norm $r^2 = |re|^2 + |im|^2 \le 2^{31}$ fits in an unsigned 32-bit integer without overflow. Simultaneously, 8-octant folding maps $\min(|re|,|im|)/\max(|re|,|im|) \in [0, 1]$ into a 2~KB \texttt{atan} table, and branchless bitwise selection (\texttt{vbslq\_u16}) resolves octants, axis boundaries, $(-32768, -32768)$, and $(0,0) \mapsto \bot$ with 0-bit scalar--SIMD parity.
    \item \textbf{Inverse Map $\Phi_{\mathrm{L}\to\mathrm{C}}: \mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{log}} \to \mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{cart}}$ (\texttt{log\_to\_cart}):} Reconstructs $(re, im)$ from $(\rho, \theta)$ via quarter-wave trigonometric tables and octave bit-shifts (4.5~KB combined). Our cohomological analysis also clarifies the fundamental limits of blind self-identification.
